# Proof strategy classification
I (and Dmitry) was curious what proof strategies are used in the [SPAR project](https://sparai.org/projects/f26/rec8qbsiN63iT3e8Z/), for both pre-training data (of which we have many different pools) and the canonical proofs in the test sets. So I had [Claude do an analysis of it](https://github.com/chainik1125/nd-rl/blob/dan/docs/papers/proof-strategies/blog.md). It's summary is almost certainly superior to mine, but let's talk through the results a little anyways.

Firstly, we can classify proof strategies. Probably obvious but worth stating. The existence of logic textbooks that teach various proof strategies again makes this kinda obvious.

Second, some strategies are much more common than others. Again, not a big surprise. It was surprising to me that the distribution of strategy likelihood was very non-uniform tho. 4 strategies showed up around 50% of the time, 1 30%, 3 10%, 3 4%, and 3 around 0.05% [source](https://github.com/chainik1125/nd-rl/blob/dan/docs/papers/proof-strategies/blog.md#what-k12-is-made-of). One of the rare strategies was 'excluded middle', and that explains why the model really struggled to pick up 'excluded-middle' in RL training (it had almost never seen it before, so it was unlikely to randomly generate it). IE, Claude found it worth mentioning when one run [here](https://github.com/chainik1125/nd-rl/blob/dan/experiment-summaries/2026-10-03-rl-continue-cap12-group-c-rises-on-3-of-3-seeds-saturation-falsified-target-burst-is-one-seeds-excluded-middle/README.md) figured out excluded-middle during RL, which did indeed result in the model somewhat suddenly improving its performance on the heldout theorem set. The heldout theorem sets often had short proofs using the strategies that were rare in pre-training ([source](https://github.com/chainik1125/nd-rl/blob/dan/docs/papers/proof-strategies/blog.md#what-textbooks-use-that-is-rare-in-k12-review)).

I like this since it means we can repeat the ['remove proof-by-contradiction' proofs experiment](https://github.com/chainik1125/nd-rl/blob/dan/experiment-summaries/2026-10-12-create-or-elicit-sigma-knockout-not-acquired-in-16-ei-rounds-needs-exposure-1pct-dose-restores/README.md) with other strategies, to gain more certainty that our results generalize.
